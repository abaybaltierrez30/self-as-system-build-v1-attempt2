Lines of Tinted Interface

This canvas tests the purple-salmon palette in constance in the interface, as well as an animated layout. The cursor controls the input, whenever the small rectangular buttons are pressed.

Reactive Dispersion Effecting

This canvas tests the latter part of the first rule (disperse and accumulate into form), and the squares reacting in movement. The cursor, once again controls the input fed into the test. After watching it run, I saw the rule was a struggle to apply, as sometimes, the click animation would not work when applying it. When I tried to give more pizzazz by making the animation do a disintegration into dust and reform, I saw a weakness there in that the disintegration part was nonfunctional for the program, so I simplified and remade it back to its original dispersing form.
